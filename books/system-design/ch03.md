# CHAPTER 3: A FRAMEWORK FOR SYSTEM DESIGN INTERVIEWS

A 4-step process for effective system design interview.

## Step 1 - Understand the problem and establish design scope

Do not jump right in to give a solution.
Slow down, think deeply and ask question to clarify requirements and assumptions.

Ask to right questions.
    - What specific featuers are we going to build ?
    - How many users does the product have ?
    - What is the company's technology stack ? What existing services you might leverage to simplify the design ?
Make assumptions
Gather all the information.

For example: Design a news feed system.

[?] Is this a mobile app or web app or both ?
    Both

[?] What are the most imporant features ?
    Make a post and see friends' news feed

[?] Is the news feed sorted in a particular order ?

[?] How many friends can a user have ?
    5000

[?] What is the traffic volume
    10 million daily active users (DAU)

[?] Can feed contains images, video, or just text
    It can contain media files, images, videos.


## Step 2 - Propose high-level design and get buy-in

Treat your interviewer as a teammate and work together.
Ask for feedback.

Draw some boxes
- clients (mobile/web)
- APIs
- web servers
- data stores
- cache
- CDN
- message queue

If possible go through a few use cases.

For example: Design a news feed system.

At the high level, the design contains 2 flows: feed publishing and news feed building

As an user, when I publish a news feed, my friends will see it.

As an user, when my friends publish news feeds, I can see them in reverse time order.
